
1. **/labs/**
   This folder stores all the hands-on practice tasks you perform for each topic. Each lab has notes, screenshots, or command outputs showing what you did. It helps prove that you actually practiced and learned, not just read theory.

2. **/runbooks/**
   Runbooks contain step-by-step guides for troubleshooting or end-to-end scenarios. They show how to handle problems methodically and are linked to recordings or evidence. Think of them as “ready-to-follow instructions” for any failure or task.

3. **/consolidations/**
   This is where you keep your consolidation notes for every task. Each note summarizes what you did, mistakes observed, corrections applied, and one transferable lesson. It ensures learning is reflected and captured, not just done.

4. **/recordings/**
   All your voice or screen recordings go here. They include timed self-interviews, lab explanations, and mock interviews. Recordings are proof that you can explain and perform tasks under time pressure.

5. **/cheatsheets/**
   This folder holds compact reference materials you can quickly glance at. Examples: one-page summaries, key commands, short definitions, or top runbooks. It’s for quick recall before interviews or practice sessions.

6. **INDEX.md**
   This file is the master tracker of your entire repo. It lists every task, its type, artifacts, pass/fail status, and date. It prevents fragmentation and acts as a single source of truth for your progress.
